The Lullaby Is Broken

Three hundred years ago, Rika, First Warden of the Ashen Vigil, descended into the catacombs with her sister Lyra. To stop the Abyssal Choir from swallowing the earth, Lyra bound her soul into the Living Lullaby, five verses that kept the Demon Lords asleep.

Now the seal is broken. Five Fallen Lords have stolen the verses into the deepest dungeons beneath the earth. Descend through five acts, open the crypts and vaults hidden along the way, and sing the Lullaby one last time.
